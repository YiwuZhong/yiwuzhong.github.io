About me

I’m a Tenure-Track Assistant Professor at Peking University, leading VaLuE Lab (Vision-Language-Embodied AI). We study how machines learn knowledge from vision, language, audio, and other modalities, and how embodied agents leverage that knowledge to act in the physical world. My recent interests span agentic robots, self-evolution in simulation, and multi-modal memory in streaming videos, 3D spaces, and robotic interactions.

I obtained my Ph.D. degree in Computer Sciences at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, advised by Prof. Yin Li. My dissertation explored how to learn visual knowledge from natural language supervision. I also had a wonderful time in the industry as a research intern: Meta AI, Microsoft Research, Tencent AI Lab at Seattle and at Shenzhen.
